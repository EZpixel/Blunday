Blunday Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

Blunday is a game made by EZpixel, an independent developer. This policy explains what information the game handles, both in the Android app and in the version that runs in a web browser.

Your game progress

Your score, gold, upgrades, achievements, game statistics (such as stars collected and platforms broken), unlocked cosmetic items and settings are saved on your own device (in the app's storage, or your browser's local storage). We do not run any servers, accounts or analytics of our own, and your progress is never sent to us.

In the Android app, if you are signed in to Google Play Games, a copy of this progress is also backed up to your Google account (see below). The web version saves progress only in your browser.

Google Play Games in the Android app

The Android app uses Google Play Games Services for sign-in, achievements, a high-score leaderboard and cloud saves. Play Games may sign you in automatically when the game starts, using the Play Games profile on your device. If you are not signed in, you can connect with Settings → Connect Play Games. Playing without signing in works exactly the same, except that your progress stays only on that device.

While you are signed in:

  • Cloud save. Your game progress (the items listed above) is stored in Google Play Games Saved Games, so it can be restored when you reinstall the game or play on another device with the same Google account. It is stored by Google in your account, not by us.
  • Achievements. Achievements you unlock are recorded on your Play Games profile. Who can see your Play Games profile and achievements is controlled by your Play Games privacy settings.
  • Leaderboard. Your best score is sent to the Blunday high-score leaderboard, including scores you set before signing in. Leaderboards show your score together with your Play Games gamer name and avatar, so other players may see them. Who can see you on leaderboards is controlled by your Play Games privacy settings.

The game does not request your name, email address, contacts or friends list. Google processes your Play Games data as described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Deleting your progress

  • Settings → Reset Progress deletes your progress on this device. If you are signed in to Play Games at the time, it also clears your cloud save.
  • If you reset while not signed in, the cloud save is kept and will be restored the next time you sign in. Other devices that have your progress saved locally may also upload it again when they next sync, so reset each device if you want a fresh start everywhere.
  • Achievements and leaderboard scores already recorded on your Play Games profile cannot be removed by the game, and Reset Progress does not remove them. You can delete your Play Games data for Blunday, or your whole Play Games profile, in the Google Play Games app or in your Google Account settings.
  • Uninstalling the app, clearing its data, or clearing your browser's site data deletes the progress stored on that device, but not your cloud save.

Ads in the Android app

The Android app occasionally offers an optional ad: after some runs you may choose to watch one to double the gold you earned. Ads are never shown unless you tap that button. The web version has no ads.

These ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect and process information from your device, such as:

  • your device's advertising ID,
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information (for example, device model, operating system and language),
  • how you interact with the ads.

This information goes directly to Google, not to us. Google's use of it is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices about ads

  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice any time with Settings → Ad Privacy Options.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your phone's settings (usually Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • You never have to watch an ad to play. The bonus is entirely optional.

Children

Blunday is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. We do not collect any personal information ourselves; the data described above is handled by Google.

Web version and hosting

The web version is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical information such as your IP address when you load the page, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. The game sets no cookies.
