Blunday Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

Blunday is a game made by EzPixel, an independent developer. This policy explains what information the game handles, both in the Android app and in the version that runs in a web browser.

Your game progress stays on your device

Your score, gold, upgrades, achievements and settings are saved only on your own device (in the app's storage, or your browser's local storage). They are never sent to us. There are no accounts, no sign-in, and we do not run any analytics or tracking of our own.

Uninstalling the app, clearing its data, or clearing your browser's site data deletes this progress. You can also delete it any time with Settings → Reset Progress.

Ads in the Android app

The Android app occasionally offers an optional ad: after some runs you may choose to watch one to double the gold you earned. Ads are never shown unless you tap that button. The web version has no ads.

These ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect and process information from your device, such as:

  • your device's advertising ID,
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information (for example, device model, operating system and language),
  • how you interact with the ads.

This information goes directly to Google, not to us. Google's use of it is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices about ads

  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice any time with Settings → Ad Privacy Options.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your phone's settings (usually Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • You never have to watch an ad to play. The bonus is entirely optional.

Children

Blunday is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. The game itself collects no personal information at all.

Web version and hosting

The web version is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical information such as your IP address when you load the page, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. The game sets no cookies.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
