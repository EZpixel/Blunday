# Blunday
A game completely vibe-coded using Fable 5.1.  Meet Blunday — a determined little creature on a mission to reach the Moon. Jump higher, dodge obstacles, collect riches, and whatever you do… **Get Rich or Fall Trying.**

### Short description

Gravity is a scam! Help a blue cube jump to the Moon. Get rich or fall trying.

### Full description

Meet Blunday, a small blue cube with big dreams and absolutely no respect for gravity.

Blunday has one goal: reach the Moon. The plan is simple. Jump higher, dodge obstacles, grab every coin in sight and, whatever you do... Get Rich or Fall Trying.

Bounce from platform to platform and climb as high as you can. Wooden platforms break under your feet, the floor is optional, and every run is a fresh shot at a new high score. Have you tried not falling?

Along the way you'll find coins, gold bars, bags of gold and red gems, which are really just angry coins. Grab jetpacks, spring boots, stars and umbrellas mid-jump to rocket past everything, although the umbrella is not rated for this altitude.

Spend your riches on upgrades with questionable names. Springy Spaghetti Legs make you bounce higher, and an Extra Thicc Fuel Tank keeps your jetpack going longer. Mary Poppins Mode lets your umbrella slow your fall even more, while Midas Stardust makes stars multiply your gold up to 10x. When things go wrong, the Ctrl+Z Jetpack saves you from certain doom. And when you're done with platforms entirely, Booster Ignition & Lift Off straps you to twin rockets that blast every run straight to 10,000.

Then hunt for achievements like "Thrust Issues", "Wood You Please Stop?", "Houston, We Have a Blunday" and "I Can Fall Now". Some of them are easy. Some of them are impossible without upgrades.

Blunday plays fair. There are no forced ads, and an ad only plays if you choose to watch one to double your gold. You don't need an account, your progress stays on your device, and runs are short enough for a quick break.
